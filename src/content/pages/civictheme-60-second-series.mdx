This site is built with the CivicTheme design system: a government-grade
component library that is accessible, inclusive and consistent.

The "CivicTheme in 60 seconds" series is a short-form video format that
introduces one part of the design system at a time — a component, a
pattern, or an accessibility feature — in about a minute each.

New episodes are announced on the [news and events](/news-and-events)
page and through the [subscribe](/subscribe) form.
